# 2026-10-23: the site never issues warnings, categories or imperatives

The wording rules in `CONTRIBUTING.md` are the operating form of this
decision.

Why: UU 24/2007 reserves early warning and disaster status for the competent
authority. UU 31/2009 Pasal 36(1) reserves meteorological, climatological
and geophysical information services for BMKG, and Pasal 89(2)(a) with PP
11/2016 Pasal 49-50 protects the public helping to disseminate BMKG
information unaltered. Air quality categories (ISPU) are KLHK's under Permen
LHK P.14/2020. A site that states sourced facts with times, relays official
statements verbatim, and compares named models stays inside what those
texts permit. A site that issues its own alert is exposed on the day it is
wrong, and a volunteer project cannot carry a missed alert on the day it
matters.

What this costs: the site will feel less decisive than an alert service.
Residents who want "is it safe to send my child to school" get the facts and
the regulation's own advice text, not a verdict.

The route to a decisive layer, if ever wanted, is a cooperation agreement
with a BPBD under which the site relays that BPBD's statements. That needs
an institution behind the project, not a change to this decision.
